How much dry ice for a bubbling Halloween cauldron.

Quick tips
  • Use hot (not boiling) water. Hotter water = more fog, but ice disappears faster.
  • Add dry ice in pieces as guests arrive so the effect starts strong.
  • Reheat or replace water when fog slows. Pellets work better than big blocks.
  • Buy the day you need it. Dry ice sublimates even in a cooler (~5–10 lb per day).
General info, not professional advice. Dry ice is extremely cold (−109°F) and can cause frostbite. Always use gloves or tongs. Use only in well-ventilated areas — CO₂ gas can displace oxygen in enclosed spaces. Never seal dry ice in a closed container. Keep away from children and pets. Not for consumption.
